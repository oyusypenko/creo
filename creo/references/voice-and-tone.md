# Voice & Tone Guidelines

Guidelines for brand voice and tone across all marketing content.

---

## Brand Voice Personality

### Who We Are to Customers

| Attribute | Description | Not This |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Expert** | Know the industry deeply | Arrogant, snobbish |
| **Helpful** | Solve problems, not sell | Pushy, aggressive |
| **Honest** | Tell the truth, even when hard | Exaggerate, hide |
| **Modern** | Current but not hype-driven | Outdated or overly trendy |
| **Human** | Speak like people, not robots | Corporate jargon |

### Voice Pillars

```
       CONFIDENT                    CLEAR                     CARING
    "We know our stuff"      "Easy to understand"      "We're here to help"
           |                        |                         |
     No hedging               No jargon                 Empathy first
     Strong claims            Simple words              Solutions focus
     Data-backed              Short sentences           Respectful tone
```

---

## Tone by Context

Voice stays constant, but **tone** changes based on context.

| Context | Tone | Do | Don't |
|---------|------|----|-------|
| Marketing Pages | Enthusiastic but grounded | "Transform your workflow" | "Revolutionary game-changing AI" |
| Documentation | Precise and instructive | "Click Upload to select a file" | "Simply click the Upload button" |
| Error Messages | Helpful and specific | "File too large. Maximum size is 2GB." | "Upload failed" |
| Email | Personal and respectful | "Your export is ready" | "YOUR EXPORT IS READY!!!" |

## Writing Principles

### 1. Lead with the Benefit
```
Bad:  "We use advanced AI technology for processing"
Good: "Get results in minutes, not hours"
```

### 2. Be Specific, Not Vague
```
Bad:  "Fast processing"
Good: "1 hour of work done in 5 minutes"
```

### 3. Use Active Voice
```
Bad:  "Results are generated by our AI"
Good: "Our AI generates results"
```

### 4. Write for Scanning
- Headlines: 6-12 words, one clear idea
- Subheadlines: Expand on the headline benefit
- Body: 2-3 sentences per paragraph max
- Bullets: Start with verbs, parallel structure

### 5. Avoid Jargon

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| "Leverage" | "Use" |
| "Utilize" | "Use" |
| "Synergy" | "Work together" |
| "Paradigm shift" | "Major change" |
| "Best-in-class" | Specific metric |

### 6. Show, Don't Tell
```
Bad:  "We have the best accuracy in the industry"
Good: "95%+ accuracy, verified by independent testing"
```

## Power Words

- **Action:** Transform, Unlock, Accelerate, Simplify, Automate, Capture, Discover, Create, Build, Launch
- **Outcome:** Instant, Accurate, Effortless, Reliable, Powerful, Professional, Seamless, Complete
- **Trust:** Secure, Private, Compliant, Certified, Proven, Trusted, Enterprise-grade

## Words to Avoid

- **Overused:** Revolutionary, Innovative, Cutting-edge, Best-in-class, State-of-the-art, Disruptive
- **Pushy:** Hurry!, Limited time!, Don't miss out!, Act now!
- **Vague:** Advanced, Sophisticated, Robust, Scalable (without specifics), Comprehensive
- **Hedging:** Perhaps, Maybe, Try to, Kind of, Sort of, Basically

---

## Formatting Guidelines

- **Headlines:** Title Case for Main Headlines
- **Subheadlines:** Sentence case for supporting text
- **Buttons:** Sentence case ("Start free trial")
- **Numbers:** Use numerals (99+, not ninety-nine+)
- **Contractions:** Use in casual content, avoid in formal/enterprise content

---

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- [ ] **Benefit-first:** Does it lead with value for the reader?
- [ ] **Specific:** Are claims backed by numbers or proof?
- [ ] **Active voice:** Is the writing direct and clear?
- [ ] **No jargon:** Would a new user understand every word?
- [ ] **Scannable:** Can someone get the point in 5 seconds?
- [ ] **On-brand:** Does it match our voice pillars?
- [ ] **Appropriate tone:** Is it right for this audience/context?
- [ ] **Consistent:** Are terms and style consistent throughout?
